About

I'm Jerry. I live in Singapore, build things with AI, and write about what I see at the frontier.

Every day, the major AI labs — OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and xAI — ship updates that quietly reshape what computers can do. Most of it gets buried in press releases, X threads, and hype cycles. This blog is my attempt to cut through that noise: one thoughtful post a day about something that actually matters.

I'm not a journalist. I'm a builder and a thinker. I care about what these tools mean for how we work, how we think, and how we live. I write with opinions, not false balance. If I think something is overhyped, I'll say it. If I think something is genuinely transformative, I'll say that too.

One post a day. No filler. Written from Singapore, usually with coffee.
