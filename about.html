Only the labs that matter.

AI Frontlines tracks the four companies shaping the frontier of artificial intelligence — OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and xAI — with zero noise from everything else.

Every day, hundreds of AI announcements flood the internet. Most are noise. Press releases padded with jargon. Funding rounds dressed as breakthroughs. Blog posts about blog posts.

AI Frontlines cuts through all of it. We track exactly four sources — the official accounts of OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind/AI, and xAI/Elon Musk — and write up what actually matters. No aggregator spam. No "startup raises seed round." Just the labs building frontier models, in their own words.

If it didn't come from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or xAI, you won't find it here.

Why these four?

As of 2026, these are the organizations training models at the frontier — the ones pushing capability boundaries, publishing research, and shipping products that millions use. When one of them announces something, the others usually respond within days. Following all four gives you the full picture of where AI is heading.

How it works

We pull directly from official X/Twitter accounts, then write editorial pieces that explain the what, the why, and the implications. Every article links back to the original source so you can verify and dive deeper.
